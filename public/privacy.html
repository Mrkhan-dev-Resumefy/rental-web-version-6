Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

At EventsRentals.io, your privacy is essential to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or book party rental equipment (such as bounce houses, outdoor movie screens, and popcorn carts) with us across Texas.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse our website, request a quote, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Full name, phone number, email address, event location/delivery city.
  • Event Details: Event date, requested rental equipment, and setup notes.
  • Analytics & Cookies: Device information, approximate location, browser type, and interaction metrics used exclusively to improve website responsiveness and customer service.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use minimal first-party and performance cookies strictly to understand site traffic patterns, monitor uptime, and maintain your rental preferences. We do not sell or trade your data to third-party ad networks.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used strictly to:

  • Confirm equipment availability and coordinate timely delivery and setup.
  • Communicate regarding your booking via phone call, SMS/text message, or WhatsApp.
  • Ensure safe, compliant equipment anchoring and power setup according to Texas safety guidelines.

4. Data Retention & Security

We maintain strict administrative safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.
